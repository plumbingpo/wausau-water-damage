Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

Wausau Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Wausau and surrounding areas. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. Our thermal imaging and moisture detection equipment traces water migration paths so every affected area is identified and properly dried, which prevents the mold growth and structural deterioration that hidden moisture causes.

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Leak detectors placed close to main supply lines and the water heater give early warning
  • Never shut the furnace off completely during a winter trip, or pipes inside may freeze
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and how to operate it quickly
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
  • Replace old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes before they fail unexpectedly
  • Monitor your water pressure, because excessive pressure (above 80 PSI) strains pipes and fittings

Signs It Is Time for Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
  • Pipes coated in ice or frost when temperatures drop
  • Water pooling on floors with no apparent source
  • Hearing water move through the house while nothing is turned on
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • A fixture that stops flowing when it is below freezing outside
  • A hidden leak often shows up first as a much larger water bill

Why Professional Burst Pipe Repair Matters

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. Our technicians find all the wet areas at the start by tracing migration with thermal imaging and meter readings.

Consumer tools cannot compare with the scale of professional drying and extraction equipment. Compared with a shop vacuum, truck-mounted extraction is hundreds of times faster, and compared with a residential dehumidifier, a commercial one removes 10 to 20 times as much moisture. Mold can begin growing in 24 to 48 hours, so this kind of power and speed matters a great deal.
