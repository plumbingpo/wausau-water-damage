Damage Prevention

Finding and Preventing Water Risks at Your Property

Owning commercial property comes with a duty of care, which means keeping the premises safe. Hidden moisture, aging plumbing, and inadequate waterproofing can result in sudden failures that cause injuries, property damage, and costly lawsuits. Wausau Restoration provides professional water damage risk assessments using moisture monitoring equipment and thermal imaging technology that evaluate every area's vulnerability to water intrusion. Findings are delivered in writing, with risks identified, priorities assigned, and specific prevention measures recommended.

These assessments serve a dual purpose: they protect people and property by identifying vulnerabilities, and they demonstrate due diligence that can protect you legally if an incident occurs. Courts and insurance companies look favorably on property owners who maintain regular professional inspections and act on expert recommendations. Our recommendation is a moisture assessment once a year for every commercial property, and more frequent checks in high-risk spots like mechanical rooms, basements, and older plumbing systems.
