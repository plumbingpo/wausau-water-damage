Mold is more than an unsightly nuisance: it is a serious health hazard and a sign of underlying moisture problems that threaten the structure of your property. Breathing it in may lead to allergy symptoms, respiratory problems, headaches and more serious effects, and the risk is highest for the elderly, the immune-compromised and children. Wausau Restoration sends IICRC-certified technicians to homes and commercial buildings across Wausau, where they contain mold, remove it and keep it from returning under strict industry protocols.

Real remediation involves a great deal more than cleaning visible growth from a wall. A professional job has five parts: locate the water source that feeds the growth, build proper containment against cross-contamination, use HEPA-filtered tools to remove damaged materials safely, treat surfaces left behind with an antimicrobial solution, and resolve the moisture problem at its root to stop recurrence. Working this way removes the mold where it starts instead of covering it up temporarily.

Each project starts with a careful inspection, and in many cases we test the air to learn which mold spores are present and at what concentration. Those findings shape a plan written for your situation instead of a generic one. IICRC S520 standards guide the whole job, which is done inside negative-pressure containment with HEPA air scrubbers running. A final verification step, which can include a clearance air test on request, shows that the space meets the standard for safe occupancy.

Maintenance Tips for Mold Remediation

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Mold-resistant paint and wallboard are the better choice wherever moisture is common, such as a basement or bath
  • Aim for 30 to 50% indoor humidity and never let it stay above 60%, running dehumidifiers and ventilating as needed
  • Hang or launder wet fabrics promptly and wipe up spills as they happen
  • A vapor barrier is worth considering in a crawl space, which also needs correct ventilation
  • Mold can get going after just 24 to 48 hours of dampness, so have leaks fixed within 24 hours
  • Check that exhaust fans in the kitchen, laundry room and bathrooms move enough air

Why Mold Remediation Is Not a DIY Job

Mold remediation is not a DIY project. Disturbing mold colonies without proper containment sends millions of spores airborne, which can contaminate unaffected areas of your home and create worse health exposure than the original problem. Professional mold remediation uses negative air pressure containment, HEPA-filtered air scrubbers and personal protective equipment so spores are captured and contained during removal.

Just as important, we deal with moisture, the underlying cause of every mold problem. If the moisture is left alone, taking away what you can see only buys time, and regrowth often shows up within weeks. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped to track down moisture you cannot see, dry the affected areas the right way and make the corrections that keep mold from recurring.

Signs It Is Time for Mold Remediation

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Belongings in storage, including clothes and cardboard, showing spots or fuzz
  • Headaches, allergy flare-ups or breathing symptoms that ease whenever you leave the house
  • Black, green, white or other colored growth you can see on surfaces in the building
  • Windows, cold surfaces or walls that sweat with condensation on a regular basis
  • Stains spreading on a ceiling or wall, or dark marks ringing the air vents
  • Peeling wallpaper, bubbling paint or warped materials that point to hidden moisture
  • A flood or leak in the recent past that never got a professional dry-out
