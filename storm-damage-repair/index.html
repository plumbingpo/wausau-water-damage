How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • In areas that see hurricanes, fit storm shutters or windows rated for impact
  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage
  • Have your roof checked annually and fix vulnerable areas ahead of the stormy months
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • Ice dams form when attics run warm, so check that yours has enough insulation and airflow

Signs It Is Time for Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
  • Rooms that start to smell stale or moldy following a storm
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard
  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • Rain blown in around door frames, window frames or openings in the wall
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow
